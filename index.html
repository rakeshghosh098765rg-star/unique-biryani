Our Story

👨‍🍳 About Unique Biryani

Welcome to Unique Biryani & Restaurant. We started with one mission: to serve the most authentic, aromatic, and comforting Biryani crafted with hand-ground spices and fresh meats.

Every handi is dum-cooked slowly over a sealed flame so that every grain of rice absorbs the rich essence of saffron, spices, and soft melt-in-the-mouth potatoes.
