👨‍🍳 About Unique Biryani

Welcome to Unique Biryani & Restaurant. We serve authentic, aromatic, and comforting Dum Biryani crafted with hand-ground spices and fresh ingredients.

Every handi is dum-cooked slowly over a sealed flame so that every grain of rice absorbs the rich essence of saffron and spices.
